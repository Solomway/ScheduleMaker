Tutorial

  1. To begin, sign in or create an account to access your data, or click on Continue as Guest.
  2. Click on the 'Edit Employees' button to add, remove, and view your list of employees.
  3. Once finished, click on the 'Edit Shifts' button to add, remove, and view your list of shifts.
  4. To create your schedule, click on the 'Edit Schedule' button, then select your starting date.
  5. Enter the number of days the schedule will span, and finally click on 'Generate Schedule'.
